MULTIPOR system

Facade insulation with MULTIPOR mineral boards

For facade insulation XLANA uses MULTIPOR mineral insulation boards made by Xella.

MULTIPOR board properties

01

Mineral material

Boards made from lime, sand, cement and water.

02

Non-combustible

Class A1 according to DIN EN 13501-1.

03

Vapour-permeable

Diffusion-open and capillary-active: they take up moisture and release it again.

04

Fibre-free

A solid, homogeneous material without fibres.

05

Against algae and fungi

Protection without biocides: moisture does not stay on the surface.

06

Eco-friendly

Fully recyclable. Free of harmful volatile substances (VOC, eco-INSTITUT label).

Board properties are based on data from the manufacturer, Xella.

MULTIPOR

How the system is built

01

Adhesive mortar

The boards are bonded to the wall with Multipor lightweight mortar.

02

MULTIPOR boards

Mineral insulation boards.

03

Reinforcement layer

Mortar with reinforcing mesh.

04

Finish

Decorative render and facade paint.

We also work with Xella materials: YTONG and SILKA.
