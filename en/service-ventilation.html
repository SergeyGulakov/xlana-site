VT501 heat recovery ventilator

What makes this ventilation different?

Conventional ventilation loses the warmth and coolness you create indoors with your climate systems. These “heat losses” significantly increase your electricity bills.

Unlike conventional systems, the VT501 heat recovery ventilator has a ceramic core that keeps the temperature stable. It sharply reduces heat loss by recovering energy from the exhaust air.

Example
Inside+25°C
Outside+35°C
Fresh air coming in27–28°C

The incoming fresh air is pre-cooled by the ceramic core. Cooling costs drop significantly, and the air comes in without dust and insects.
